# Asset provenance

Version 1.1.0 · 28 September 2026

## Portrait and documentary photography

`assets/portrait-cutout.webp` is the locally bundled homepage portrait; `assets/portrait-studio.jpg` is retained for the expanded profile. `assets/at-work-*.jpg` and `assets/field-workshop-wall.jpg` are local documentary images supplied in the user's design archive. They are used as editorial evidence plates and described by their local captions. The site does not add institution logos or imply endorsement. These images are not used to claim an outcome beyond their visible subject and caption.

## Cinematic 3D model and charts

The pinned homepage film uses 48 CSS 3D fragments and six arrangements generated in `src/site.js`. Local photo prints and explicitly labelled charts/model sheets are overlays. The 400-firm, 120-interview, eight-focus-group chart and USD 44.108 million project-budget sheet are drawn from the supplied CV evidence extraction; labels distinguish scope from impact or money managed. `assets/evidence-sculpture.webp` is retained as an unused legacy generated asset; it is not the current hero or a documentary project image.

Prompt: A high-resolution fine-art studio still life, an abstract physical sculpture expressing scattered evidence becoming a coherent structure. Seamless warm ivory #F6F1E6 background and tabletop. Very thin ivory paper and board planes, offset and fragmented at the left, resolving at the right into orderly intersecting forest-green #1E3D2F translucent planes. One crisp cobalt #1F44C0 line threads through the composition, with a single small muted amber #C07A08 intervention point. Museum-quality tangible materials, orthographic architectural macro photography, subtle paper fibres and exact cut edges. Landscape 3:2, entire assembly visible with generous negative space. Hard raking sunlight from upper left and rich cast shadows. No text, people, interface, logos, watermarks, stars, spheres, particles, neon or lens flares.

Generated once through ImageGen and inspected before integration. No generated portrait, institutional logo or project documentary photo was used.

## Typography

Self-hosted variable WOFF2 files: Anēk Latin, Anēk Bangla, Newsreader normal/italic and Martian Mono. Licenses are included in `assets/font-licenses/`. `prepare-fonts.mjs` is optional and is not part of normal reconstruction.

## Diagrams and icon

The Decision Flow graphics are schematic evidence-to-system diagrams, implemented with semantic SVG paths and a shared set of CSS 3D data fragments. Apart from charts carrying their cited scope labels, diagrams do not represent measured project data. The favicon is a typographic “sh” monogram. There are no external image hotlinks or institution logos.
